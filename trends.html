Each point is one year's summary statistic, calculated the same way as on the daily page: every day's high or low compared with NOAA's 1991–2020 normal for that date. Click a point to see that year day by day.

Data: NOAA daily observations and 1991–2020 daily normals from the ACIS web service (data.rcc-acis.org), for the city's official climate station. Years with fewer than 300 days of complete readings are left out. Trend lines are ordinary least-squares fits over the complete years shown. Because every year is compared with the same 1991–2020 normals, years before 1991 tend to sit below zero and recent years above it when a station has warmed.
